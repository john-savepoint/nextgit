# Fork Community + Intent Router (post-demo; locked as platform direction)

**Job to be done:** *"Watch my intent against everyone else's work and tell me when to join instead of build."* Join-don't-restart (R4) as a platform reflex — not a social convention.

## The intent router

Declare intent first → the platform matches it against the fork ecosystem → **"80% done by fork X"** pings *before* the work begins.

## Matcher verdicts

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **merge it** | their work does the job — direct merge recommendation |
| **use as context** | partial fit — build with their implementation in hand; LLM-merge/refactor economics apply |
| **ignore** | different goal |

## Fork-card signals (six clusters; triggers investigation, never auto-decision)

1. **Fit / overlap** — semantic alignment between my declared intent and theirs
2. **People / trust** — signed identity; personal + community reputation
3. **Liveness** — velocity, recency, self-gardening
4. **Proof** — tests, receipts, session trail
5. **Distance** — drift from my base; conflict surface
6. **Cost-to-adopt vs regenerate** — LLM-merge estimate

## Mechanisms

- **Active-fork radar** — surface active, relevant forks in context; see their unique diff vs yours; fork-off-fork collaboration; more merge-back to upstream.
- **Background comparison sub-agent** — continuously diffs another fork's work against our PRD/prompt/implementation/goal; returns the verdict shape above.

## Deferred (open)

- Partial-fit operational policy (the *use as context* branch mechanics) — left open by the user; needs trenches time.
- Concrete fork-card design (which of the six clusters render, and how) — not decided.
