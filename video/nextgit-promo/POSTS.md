## LinkedIn

> **Five AI agents. One repo.** That's the new normal — and review is the bottleneck.
>
> We built NextGit around a simple bet: agent work should land as ONE comparable set, not five diffs you read one at a time.
>
> In 86 seconds, the cut shows the whole loop on the real platform:
> • every proposal arrives with five server-side checks — fold, collision, conflicts, secrets, base
> • a judge scores the set (and skips the secret-class one, on purpose)
> • secret files stay typed — key names, never values; reveal is audited and repo-scoped
> • every landing ships with a signed attestation you can verify yourself
> • review stays social: comments on the merge, a rail that tells the set's story
>
> Nothing in the video is a mockup — every frame is a capture of the live deployment.
